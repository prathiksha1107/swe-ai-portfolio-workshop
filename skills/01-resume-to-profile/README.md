# Resume to Profile

## What this skill does

This skill helps an AI assistant turn an existing resume into the structured
`PROFILE.md` used by the workshop portfolio templates. It extracts facts, points out
missing information, and then stops so you can check the result.

## What you need

- An updated resume in PDF, DOCX, Markdown, or readable text.
- One of the workshop portfolio templates.
- An AI assistant that can read your resume and repository files, such as Codex.

Your uploaded **source resume** can have any filename. A resume you choose to make
downloadable on the public website is different: put that file at
`assets/resume.pdf`. Publishing a resume is optional.

## How to use it

1. Add or upload your resume.
2. Ask your AI assistant to use `skills/01-resume-to-profile/SKILL.md`.
3. Let the AI populate the selected template's `PROFILE.md`.
4. Answer its short follow-up questions.
5. Read all of `PROFILE.md` yourself.
6. Correct anything inaccurate and decide what contact information may be public.
7. Only after verification, continue to the future Portfolio Builder skill.

**Never allow AI to invent career information.** Your workflow is: AI extracts,
you verify, and only then does AI build the public portfolio.
